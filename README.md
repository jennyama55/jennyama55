## About me:

What eventually pulled me toward AI was realizing it offered something math alone didn't — room to be creative. AI gave me structure to work within, but also space to experiment, iterate, and build something that didn't exist before. That mix of rigor and imagination is what keeps me interested.
 
I'm currently studying Math of Computation at UCLA with a minor in Data Science Engineering, and I'm a Break Through Tech AI Fellow, where I've been building out my ML skill set through hands-on projects. I care a lot about doing things carefully and thoroughly — whether that's a proof, a model, or a project I'm building for fun.
 
I'm not 100% sure yet where this leads (grad school for CS is on the table, among other paths), but I'm excited to keep exploring the space between logic and creativity.
